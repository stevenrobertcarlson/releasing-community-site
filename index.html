How it works

  • Everything happens on WhatsApp, with calls on Zoom. You will need WhatsApp to join the groups.
  • The 24/7 On-Call group is for people already trained in the Sedona Method. Everyone is welcome on the support calls and in the Sedona Support group.
  • This is peer support. It is not therapy and not a crisis service. If you are in crisis, please contact local emergency services or a crisis line.

Offered freely. Hosted by Sedona Method coaches and longtime practitioners, many of them certified.

How to join the Releasing Community

Enter your name and email. It helps keep out spam. You'll get five short orientation emails.

Check your inbox for a confirmation email

After you confirm, you'll get the WhatsApp group links and a guide to the 24/7 On-Call Releasing group. If nothing arrives, check your spam folder and add the sender to your contacts.
